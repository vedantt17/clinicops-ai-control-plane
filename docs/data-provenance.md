# Data Provenance and Licensing

All records are generated in `lib/fhir-fixtures.ts` and `lib/workflow-engine.ts`. They are deterministic, synthetic, and contain no patient names, birth dates, addresses, contact details, clinical notes, or external healthcare records.

FHIR resource shapes are a small educational subset of the HL7 FHIR R4 specification. This repository does not redistribute a proprietary EHR dataset, payer portal content, or regulated health information.

Operational volumes, handling times, vendor scores, model costs, and capacity figures are scenario inputs for demonstration. They must not be represented as Flagler Health, customer, employer, or realized business outcomes.
